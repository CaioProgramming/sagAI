# Sagas

**Sagas is a casual solo RPG on your phone — you write the turns, the world responds, and you walk
away feeling like you lived through an adventure that was yours alone.**

Pick a vibe — fantasy, cyberpunk, horror, superheroes, crime, space opera, shinobi, and more. Create
your character, set the opening scene, and start playing by writing: what you say, what you think,
what you do, what happens around you, who shows up next. The story grows from there — chapters,
events, a cast of characters — and the whole app shifts mood to match the genre you chose.

There is no script to prepare and no grind. Drop in when you feel like it, send a turn, read what
happened, send another. Be the hero, the villain, the chaotic mess, the dramatic one — whatever fits
the moment.

When a saga wraps up, you get a **retrospective** of how it went — your style, the characters who
stuck around, the emotional arc of the journey. Finished sagas sit on a **trophy shelf** at home,
like little souvenirs of adventures you actually played through.

---

## How you play

1. **Create a saga** — choose a genre, describe your character, and set where the story begins.
2. **Take turns** — write dialogue, thoughts, actions, narration, new characters, or new chapters.
3. **Let it grow** — the world keeps responding; your saga builds chapters, events, and lore over
   time.
4. **Come back anytime** — no pressure, no schedule. Play in short bursts on the couch or go deep
   when you are in the mood.

## What defines Sagas

- **Improvised adventure, game feel** — not a blank page, not a rigid quest log. Just "what if I did
  this?" and see where it goes.
- **Genre as atmosphere** — each universe has its own visual identity and tone baked into the
  experience.
- **Your journey, remembered** — retrospectives and the trophy shelf turn finished stories into
  something you can look back on, not just close and forget.

## Development

Sagas is an Android app in active development. The codebase covers saga creation, turn-based chat
gameplay, chapter and timeline management, character galleries, retrospectives, and genre-themed UI.

### Build

Standard Android Gradle project — open in Android Studio and run the `app` module on a device or
emulator.
